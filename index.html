About the Jam

We welcome players of all levels to join us for old time tunes on unamplified fiddles, banjos, guitars, basses, or anything you think sounds good.

  • Keys. Most of our tunes are in G, A, C, or D, so bring a capo if you need one.
  • Pace. We often play through tunes slowly a few times to help everyone get the hang of it. No pressure, just fun.
  • Potluck. Feel free to bring food or drinks to share. It is totally optional.
  • Access. The house is wheelchair accessible, with a ramp and an accessible bathroom.

We are all about keeping it friendly, safe, and welcoming.
